A legacy rooted in trust.

From a family enterprise in the pre-independence era to one of South India’s most dependable food processing groups.

B. S. Amaranarayana GuptaFounder
(01) Founder’s legacy

A vision that nourishes generations.

Grounded in tradition and propelled by purpose — a dedication to quality, authenticity and trust in every product that reaches the consumer’s home.

The roots of this legacy were laid in the pre-independence era by Shri. B. S. Seetharam Shetty, whose family business was built on integrity, hard work and community-driven enterprise. Carrying these values forward, Shri. B. S. Amaranarayana Gupta formally established Sri Bhagyalakshmi Industries and steadily expanded into pulse, rice and wheat processing — foreseeing the shifting demands of an increasingly urban India.

What began as a small venture has evolved into a well-regarded food brand offering more than 110 trusted products, with a solid footprint across South India.

VISION

To become one of India’s most trusted and respected food brands, offering world-class quality supported by a strong nationwide footprint.

MISSION

To enhance food quality through innovation and technology-driven processes; to establish a robust distribution network; and to provide dependable, cost-effective, top-notch products without sacrificing safety or trust.

(04) Group ecosystem

An integrated network built for growth.

Specialised processing, trading, agro and infrastructure companies — delivering sourcing, processing, distribution, retail, HoReCa and exports end-to-end.

Supply Foundation

Core Trading & Raw Material Procurement

The commercial backbone of the SBL network

Amar FoodsStrengthens the raw-material ecosystem through procurement of high-quality paddy directly from key farming regions, fostering long-term relationships with farming communities.
Production Engine

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Commercial CorporationProcessing cereals, pulses, dals, gram flour and edible oils for supply across South India and beyond.
