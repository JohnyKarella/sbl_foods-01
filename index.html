(01) Founder’s legacy

A vision that nourishes generations

When food processing in India was still in its early stages, our founder envisioned a future where quality, consistency and trust would form the foundation of the Indian food industry. What began as a small venture is now a brand with over 110 trusted products — favoured in every third household in South India.

VISION Most trusted

To become one of India’s most trusted and respected food brands, offering world-class quality supported by a strong nationwide footprint.

MISSION Quality, reach, value

Enhance food quality through innovation and technology, build a robust distribution network, and deliver dependable, cost-effective products without sacrificing safety or trust.

Roots
1947

Foundations of a Family Enterprise

B. S. Seetharam Shetty lays the family business foundation on integrity, hard work and community.

The founding
1960

Birth of Sri Bhagyalakshmi Industries

B. S. Amaranarayana Gupta establishes Sri Bhagyalakshmi Industries — organised food processing begins.

Trading
1972

Trading Capabilities Take Shape

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Trading Corporation opens with its own fried gram unit.

Rice processing
1982

Expansion into Rice Processing

Sri Balaji Rice Industries is formed through an acquisition — large-scale rice processing begins.

Value-added rice
1985

Value-Added Rice Products Introduced

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Enterprises starts producing premium thick and thin rice flakes.

Wheat milling
1989

Foray into Wheat Milling

Sri Bhagyalakshmi Agro Foods Pvt. Ltd. begins milling maida and semolina at scale.

Fourth generation
2011

Fourth Generation Entrepreneurship

Gupta Foods is launched for trading, HoReCa and institutional food supply.

Going global
2012

Global Footprint Established

The first overseas rice processing unit opens: Nyagatare Agro Ventures, Rwanda.

Agro trading
2013

Strengthening Agro Trading

Amar Trading Company strengthens agro-product trading and market outreach.

Food park
2017

M/s Agratha Mega Food Park LLP

Agratha Mega Food Park LLP — processing units, silos, cold storage, labs and logistics in one ecosystem.

North Karnataka
2018

Shree Bhageeratha Industries

Shree Bhageeratha Industries in Dharwad processes fried gram and pulses for the region.

Central India
2019

M/s Amar Moti Agro Foods LLP

Amar Moti Agro Foods LLP in Indore processes Bansi Suji, flours and pulses at full scale.

Golden jubilee
2022

Our Golden Journey of Excellence

50 glorious years of passion and perseverance — a milestone of enduring legacy.

New categories
2024

Expanding Our Reach in Processed Foods

Sweets, snacks, ready-to-cook and ready-to-eat products join the portfolio.
